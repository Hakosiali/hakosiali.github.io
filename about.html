About AIGuideDZ

AIGuideDZ exists to answer one question: "Which tech is actually worth my time and money?"

AI moved faster in the last three years than any technology before it. Every week brings new tools, new "game-changers," and new hype. Most people — especially students and anyone on a budget — don't have time to test it all. So we do it for them.

What we publish

  • AI tutorials — step-by-step guides to tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Claude Code, written for beginners.
  • Free AI tool roundups — because the free tier is where everyone should start.
  • Tech buying guides — laptops and phones judged by the specs that matter, not marketing.
  • Productivity & study resources — prompts, workflows and apps that save real hours.
  • English-learning tech — AI has quietly become the best language tutor ever built; we show you how to use it.

Our principles

  1. We test before we recommend. If it's on a list, we've used it.
  2. Free-first. We always tell you what the free version can do before suggesting you pay.
  3. Plain English. If a guide needs a glossary, we rewrote it.
  4. Honest monetization. Some links earn us a commission — it never changes a ranking, and it's always disclosed. Read our affiliate disclosure.

Who reads us

Students building their first study stack, professionals adding AI to their workflow, English learners, and anyone buying tech on a real-world budget. If that's you — welcome.
