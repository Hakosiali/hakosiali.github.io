How to Use Google Gemini: Complete Beginner's Guide

✍️ AIGuideDZ Team📅 Updated July 2026📖 10 min read

Gemini is Google's AI assistant — and if you have a Google account, you already have it. It answers questions, writes drafts, generates images, summarizes YouTube videos, and works inside Gmail, Docs and Android. This guide takes you from zero to using its best features, free.

Getting started (2 minutes)

  1. Go to gemini.google.com and sign in with any Google account — or on Android, hold the power button / say "Hey Google" (Gemini is the default assistant on most recent phones).
  2. That's it. The free tier needs no card and no separate signup, and its limits are among the most generous in AI.
  3. On iPhone: install the Google Gemini app from the App Store.

Students: check whether your university Google account includes premium Gemini features via Google Workspace for Education — many do, free.

Your first prompts

Everything from our prompt-writing guide applies to Gemini: give it a role, a task, context and a format. Three starters that show its range:

  • "Explain [topic from your course] like I'm 15, then give me 5 quiz questions to test myself."
  • "Summarize this YouTube video and list its 3 main arguments: [paste link]" — a Gemini specialty.
  • "Create an image of [description], photorealistic, warm lighting."

The 6 free features worth knowing

1. Answers grounded in Google Search

Gemini can check the live web and cite sources — ask it to "search for recent information" when the topic is current. Still verify sources before citing them anywhere real.

2. YouTube video summaries

Paste any YouTube link and ask for a summary, the key timestamps, or "what does this video claim and is it accurate?". Enormous time-saver for lecture recordings and tutorials.

3. Image generation and editing

Generate images from text free (with limits), or upload a photo and ask for edits — background changes, style transfers, object removal. Gemini's photorealism is among the best free options; see how it compares in our image generator roundup.

4. Deep Research

Ask for a researched report on any topic and Gemini browses dozens of sources and compiles a structured document with citations. Ideal starting point for essays, market overviews and buying decisions — as a starting point, then verify.

5. File uploads

Upload PDFs, images or spreadsheets and ask questions about them. For studying from your own materials long-term, Google's NotebookLM is the better dedicated tool.

6. Gems (custom assistants)

Create reusable assistants with standing instructions — a "study coach" that always quizzes you, a "proofreader" with your style rules. Free, and it saves retyping the same setup prompt.

Gemini in Gmail & Docs

This is Gemini's unfair advantage — availability varies by account type, but where present:

  • Gmail: "Summarize this thread", "Draft a polite reply declining this", "Find the email with the invoice from May".
  • Docs: "Help me write" generates and rewrites text in place; ask for outlines, tone changes, shorter versions.
  • Sheets: generate tables and formulas from descriptions — our AI spreadsheet guide covers the same skills for Excel.
  • Drive: ask questions across your own documents.

Settings worth changing

  • Gemini Apps Activity: review how your conversations are used and set auto-delete (3/18/36 months) at myactivity.google.com. Human reviewers may see sampled chats unless you opt out — don't paste anything truly private.
  • Extensions/connections: control which Google apps Gemini can reach (Gmail, Drive, Maps, YouTube) — turn off what you don't want it touching.
  • Language: Gemini works well in French and Arabic too — just write to it in your language.

What Gemini is bad at (honestly)

  • Long-form writing quality: drafts lean generic; Claude writes noticeably better prose — see Claude vs Gemini.
  • Sustained coding work: fine for snippets and explanations; for real projects, Claude Code is a different league.
  • Confident mistakes: like every AI, it states wrong things fluently. Facts, numbers and citations need checking.

FAQ

Is Gemini really free? Yes — the free tier is generous and includes image generation, file uploads and Deep Research (with usage limits). Google AI Pro (~$20/month) raises limits and adds premium models and Drive storage.

Gemini or ChatGPT? Full breakdown in our ChatGPT vs Gemini comparison — short version: Gemini if you live in Google apps, ChatGPT for the most polished general chat.

Can teachers/schools tell I used it? Assume yes, and use it legitimately — our AI essay-writing guide shows the workflow that improves your work without crossing lines.
