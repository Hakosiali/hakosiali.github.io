How to Use Perplexity AI: The Search Engine That Cites Its Sources

✍️ AIGuideDZ Team📅 Updated August 2026📖 9 min read

Perplexity solves a specific frustration: you ask ChatGPT a factual question and get a confident answer with no way to check where it came from. Perplexity was built around the opposite idea — every answer is a synthesized summary with numbered citations you can click through to the original source. It's less a chatbot and more a research assistant that shows its work.

Getting started (2 minutes)

  1. Go to perplexity.ai and start asking questions — a free account (email or Google sign-in) unlocks more daily searches and saved history.
  2. Install the free iOS/Android app, or the browser extension that lets you search directly from your address bar.
  3. That's the whole setup. There's no separate "search mode" to enable — every answer is sourced by default.

The short version: use Google when you know what site you want. Use ChatGPT or Claude for writing, coding and general help. Use Perplexity when the question is "what's actually true about X" and you might need to show someone where you got the answer.

The features worth knowing

Focus modes

Narrow a search to specific source types: Academic (scholarly papers), Reddit (community opinions), or the open web. Switching to Academic mode for a research question filters out blog-spam and prioritizes real papers.

Pro Search

For complex questions, Pro Search breaks the query into sub-questions, searches each separately, and combines the findings — closer to the "Deep Research" modes in Gemini and ChatGPT, but built around citations from the ground up.

Image and file understanding

Upload a photo, chart, or document and ask questions about it, same as the major chatbots — useful for "what does this graph show, and can you find supporting data online?"

Spaces: organize research by project

A Space is a dedicated folder for an ongoing research task — a thesis, a purchase decision, a work project — where every search inside it stays grouped together and can reference uploaded files specific to that project. If you're comparing products, planning a trip, or researching a paper over several days, starting a Space keeps everything in one place instead of scattered across your search history.

What Perplexity is bad at (honestly)

  • Not a writing tool. It can draft text, but Claude and ChatGPT produce noticeably better long-form writing — Perplexity's strength is finding and citing, not composing.
  • Not for coding. It can look up documentation, but it's not built for the agentic, multi-file work covered in our coding assistants guide.
  • Sources aren't automatically reliable. A citation proves where an answer came from, not that the source itself is accurate — you still need to judge the source the way you would in any research.

FAQ

Is Perplexity better than Google?

For research questions with a clear answer, often yes — you get a synthesized summary with sources instead of ten links to click through yourself. For navigational searches (finding a specific website) or shopping, plain Google is still faster.

Is Perplexity free?

Yes, the free tier covers a generous number of searches per day using a capable model, which is enough for most students and casual researchers. Perplexity Pro (~$20/month) adds unlimited searches and access to stronger models.

Perplexity or ChatGPT/Gemini's search features?

Perplexity is built around search and citation as its core product, so the source list is more prominent and reliable than search-mode add-ons in general chatbots. If citing sources matters for your work, Perplexity is the more careful choice.

💡 Pro tip: for an essay or report that needs real citations, start your research in Perplexity, then move to the legitimate essay-writing workflow to turn sourced facts into your own argument — don't paste Perplexity's summary directly into your paper.
