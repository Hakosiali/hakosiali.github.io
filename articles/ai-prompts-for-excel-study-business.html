50+ AI Prompts for Excel, Study & Business (Copy-Paste Ready)

✍️ AIGuideDZ Team📅 Updated July 2026📖 12 min read

The difference between "AI is useless" and "AI saved me three hours today" is almost always the prompt. Vague question in, vague answer out. This page is a library of specific, tested prompts you can copy, fill in the [brackets], and use in ChatGPT, Claude or any capable AI assistant.

💡 The golden rule of prompting: give the AI a role, a task, context, and a format. "You are an Excel expert (role). Write a formula (task) for this data layout: … (context). Explain it step by step (format)."

📊 Excel & spreadsheet prompts

  1. "You are an Excel expert. My sheet has [column A: dates, column B: product names, column C: sales amounts]. Write a formula that [sums sales for each month]. Explain how it works."
  2. "Explain the difference between VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP and INDEX-MATCH with a simple example of each. When should I use which?"
  3. "This formula returns an error: [paste formula]. My data looks like: [describe]. What's wrong and how do I fix it?"
  4. "Design a spreadsheet layout to track [monthly personal budget] with categories, planned vs actual, and a summary dashboard. List the columns, formulas and one chart to include."
  5. "Write a formula to clean this data: [describe mess, e.g. names in ALL CAPS with extra spaces]. Then show the same fix using Power Query steps."
  6. "Create sample data (20 rows) I can paste into Excel to practice pivot tables, then give me 5 pivot table exercises with answers."
  7. "I have [two sheets: orders and customers, linked by customer ID]. Write the formula to pull each customer's city into the orders sheet."
  8. "Turn this repetitive task into a step-by-step Excel macro: [describe task]. Give me the VBA code with comments explaining each line."

🎓 Studying & exam prep prompts

  1. "Explain [photosynthesis] in three levels: to a 10-year-old, to a high school student, and to a university student. I'll tell you which level matched my understanding."
  2. "Here are my lecture notes: [paste]. Create a one-page summary with the 10 most exam-relevant points, then 5 likely exam questions with model answers."
  3. "Quiz me on [topic]. Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer, tell me if I'm right, and get harder as I improve. Track my score."
  4. "I have [14 days] before an exam on [subjects/chapters]. I can study [2 hours/day]. Build me a day-by-day revision plan with spaced repetition — revisit each topic at growing intervals."
  5. "I'll explain [concept] in my own words. Point out every mistake or gap in my explanation, then give me a corrected version: [your explanation]."
  6. "Create 15 flashcards (question on one line, answer on the next) from this text: [paste]. Focus on definitions, dates and cause-effect relationships."
  7. "Compare and contrast [concept A] and [concept B] in a table: definition, when it applies, strengths, weaknesses, one example each."
  8. "I got this exam question wrong: [question + your answer]. Explain the correct approach step by step, then give me 3 similar practice questions."

✍️ Essay & writing prompts

  1. "I'm writing an essay on [topic]. Suggest 3 possible thesis statements with a one-line argument outline for each. Ask me questions first if the topic is too broad."
  2. "Critique this draft like a strict professor: [paste]. Focus on argument logic and structure, not grammar. List the 3 weakest points and how to fix them."
  3. "Rewrite this paragraph to be clearer and 30% shorter without losing meaning: [paste]."
  4. "Here's my outline: [paste]. What counterarguments am I ignoring? Give the strongest objection to my thesis and how I could address it."
  5. "Check this text for flow: point out every sentence where a reader might get confused or lose the thread, and explain why: [paste]."

💼 Business & email prompts

  1. "Write a professional email to [a client] about [a project delay]. Tone: apologetic but confident. Include a new timeline and one goodwill gesture. Keep it under 150 words."
  2. "Turn these rough meeting notes into structured minutes with decisions, action items (with owners) and open questions: [paste]."
  3. "I run a [small phone repair shop]. Give me 10 concrete ideas to increase revenue in the next 60 days, ranked by effort vs impact."
  4. "Draft a one-page business plan for [idea]: problem, solution, target customer, revenue model, 3 biggest risks, first 5 steps to launch."
  5. "Summarize this report for my manager in 5 bullet points with one recommended decision: [paste]."
  6. "Act as a tough negotiator on the other side of this deal: [describe]. I'll make my offer, you respond realistically, and after each round tell me what I could have said better."
  7. "Write a polite follow-up to an unanswered email. Version 1: after 3 days. Version 2: after 2 weeks (final). Context: [paste original email]."

📣 Marketing & social media prompts

  1. "Generate 10 blog post titles about [topic] optimized for search. Mix how-to, listicle and comparison formats. Then mark the 3 with the best click-through potential and explain why."
  2. "Write 5 Instagram captions for [product/post], each with a different hook style: question, bold claim, statistic, story, humor. Include hashtag suggestions."
  3. "Create a 30-day content calendar for a [tech tips] page: post idea, format (reel/carousel/text), and goal (reach/engagement/sales) for each day."
  4. "Rewrite this product description to focus on benefits instead of features, for an audience of [students]: [paste]."
  5. "Analyze this landing page copy and list what's stopping visitors from converting, in priority order: [paste]."

🗣️ English-learning prompts

  1. "Let's have a conversation about [daily life topic]. Correct my mistakes in brackets after each of my messages, but keep the conversation flowing."
  2. "Give me 10 sentences with common B1-level grammar mistakes. I'll correct them; then grade me and explain the rules I struggled with."
  3. "Teach me 8 phrasal verbs used in [job interviews], each with an example dialogue line and a mini-exercise."
  4. "I'll write a paragraph in English. Rewrite it three ways: casual, professional, academic — and highlight what changed: [paste]."
  5. "Simulate a job interview for [position] in English. Ask one question at a time. After each answer, give feedback on both content and language."

How to get better results from any prompt

  • Add examples. "Here's one I like: [example]. Match this style" improves output instantly.
  • Iterate. The first answer is a draft. Say "shorter", "more formal", "give me 3 alternatives" — refining is where the magic happens.
  • Ask it to ask you. Ending with "ask me anything you need to know first" prevents generic answers.
  • Set constraints. Word counts, formats, and "do not" rules keep answers on target.
