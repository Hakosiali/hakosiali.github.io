How to Use AI in Excel: Copilot, ChatGPT & Free Ways

✍️ AIGuideDZ Team📅 Updated July 2026📖 10 min read

Excel is where AI saves the most obvious time. Nobody enjoys memorizing formula syntax, untangling nested IFs, or cleaning a column of inconsistent dates — and AI is genuinely excellent at all three. This guide covers the three ways to use AI with Excel, from completely free to fully integrated, with copy-paste prompts for the most common jobs.

The 3 ways to use AI with Excel

  1. Free chatbot alongside Excel (start here). Keep ChatGPT or Claude open in a browser tab. Describe what you want, paste sample data, copy the formula back. Zero cost, works with any Excel version — and honestly covers 80% of what most people need.
  2. Upload the file to the chatbot. Both ChatGPT and Claude accept spreadsheet uploads on free tiers (limits apply). The AI can then see your actual columns, write formulas that match, summarize the data, and even produce charts. Best for one-off analysis of non-sensitive data.
  3. Microsoft Copilot inside Excel. A paid add-on that lives in the ribbon: highlight data, ask questions, get formulas, formatting and pivot tables in place. Most convenient, least free — verdict below.

Google Sheets users: Gemini does the Copilot job there, and many education/workspace accounts include it — see our Gemini comparison.

Writing formulas from plain English

The core skill is describing the goal, your columns, and the edge cases. The difference between a wrong formula and a right one is almost always the context you give.

Copy-paste prompt: "Write an Excel formula. My data: column A = order date, column B = customer name, column C = amount. I want: total of column C for orders in March 2026 only. My Excel version supports dynamic arrays. Explain the formula briefly so I can adapt it."

Three habits that prevent 90% of formula frustration:

  • Name your columns in the prompt. "Column A = order date" beats "my dates" every time.
  • Mention your Excel version (or say "must work in older Excel") — modern functions like XLOOKUP and FILTER don't exist in old versions.
  • Paste 3–5 rows of sample data (anonymized) so the AI sees the real formats it's dealing with.

When a formula errors, paste the formula and the error message back and ask what's wrong — AI is even better at debugging formulas than writing them.

Cleaning messy data

The least glamorous, highest-value use. Things AI handles in seconds that used to eat afternoons:

  • Dates in mixed formats ("3/4/26", "March 4", "04-03-2026") → one consistent format
  • Names in one column → split into first/last, with proper capitalization
  • Extra spaces, inconsistent phone formats, duplicated rows → flagged and fixed
  • Text categories with typos ("Marketing", "marketng", "MKT") → standardized

Copy-paste prompt: "Here are 10 sample rows from a messy column: [paste]. Give me either an Excel formula or step-by-step Flash Fill / Power Query instructions to standardize the whole column to [format]. Prefer the simplest method."

Analyzing data & building charts

Upload a spreadsheet (or paste a table) and ask questions in plain language: What are the trends? Which product is declining? Summarize this by month and region. Both ChatGPT and Claude will compute answers and generate charts; Claude is particularly good at explaining what the numbers mean and building interactive summaries from your file.

The prompt that impresses your boss: "Here's our sales data. Analyze it like a business analyst: 3 key trends, 2 concerns, 1 opportunity — each backed by specific numbers. Then suggest the single chart that best shows the main story, and create it."

Always sanity-check totals against the sheet itself before presenting — AI arithmetic on uploaded files is good but not infallible.

Is Microsoft Copilot worth paying for?

You…Verdict
Use Excel a few times a week❌ Skip it — the free chatbot-in-a-tab workflow covers you
Live in Excel daily (finance, ops, analysts)✅ Often worth it — in-place formulas, pivots and formatting add up
Already pay for Microsoft 365 with Copilot included via work✅ Use it — it's free to you
Work mostly in Google SheetsUse Gemini there instead

Rule of thumb: pay for AI inside the spreadsheet only when the copy-paste hop to a free chatbot happens often enough to annoy you daily.

A word on private data

Before uploading any spreadsheet to a chatbot: remove or anonymize personal data (names, emails, salaries, customer details) and check your employer's policy. Company financials in a personal chatbot account is how people get fired. Enterprise versions of these tools exist precisely for that reason.
