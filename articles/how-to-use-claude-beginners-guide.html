How to Use Claude AI: Complete Beginner's Guide

✍️ AIGuideDZ Team📅 Updated July 2026📖 10 min read

Claude, made by Anthropic, is the AI most people discover once they've outgrown "just a chatbot" — it's the one writers, students and developers reach for when the work is long, careful or technical. This guide gets you from zero to using its best features, all free to start.

Getting started (2 minutes)

  1. Go to claude.ai and sign up free with an email or Google account — no card required.
  2. You land in a plain chat box. That simplicity is deliberate — Claude's interface stays out of the way of the work.
  3. Install the free iOS/Android app for the same account on mobile.

The free tier gives you a capable model with a daily message limit that resets each day — enough for most students and casual daily use.

Your first conversation

Everything in our prompt-writing guide applies here: give Claude a role, a task, context and a format. Three starters that show what it's good at:

  • "You are a strict editor. Critique this paragraph's argument and clarity, not grammar: [paste]."
  • "Explain [a concept from your course] like I'm new to the subject, then quiz me with 3 questions."
  • "Here's a long document: [paste or upload]. Summarize the 5 most important points for someone who won't read it."

Claude tends to give more careful, hedged answers than other assistants — if it says it's uncertain, that's a feature, not evasiveness.

Projects: organize work with shared context

A Project is a persistent workspace: add background documents once (a syllabus, a style guide, a codebase overview), and every conversation inside that Project automatically has that context — no re-explaining your situation each time. Set one up per class, per client, or per ongoing piece of writing.

Example: a "Thesis" Project with your research notes and outline uploaded once; every chat inside it already knows your argument and sources.

Artifacts: documents, code and mini-apps

Ask Claude to write an essay, build a webpage, or draft a spreadsheet, and it can open the result in a separate panel called an Artifact — editable, re-runnable, and easy to iterate on without scrolling back through chat history. For code specifically, Artifacts render working previews live. This is also the foundation behind Claude Code, the terminal tool for larger software projects.

Uploading files

Drop in PDFs, Word docs, spreadsheets or images and ask questions directly — Claude is particularly strong with long documents, handling hundreds of pages in one go without losing track of earlier sections. Good uses: "find every clause about termination in this contract," "check this spreadsheet's totals against these rules," "what does this chart actually show."

Anonymize personal or confidential data before uploading, same as with any AI chatbot — see the privacy note in our AI in Excel guide for the general rule.

Settings worth knowing

  • Custom instructions/preferences: tell Claude once how you like answers formatted (concise, bullet points, no fluff) and it applies that across every new chat.
  • Data controls: review what conversation data is retained and for how long in your account settings — don't paste anything truly sensitive regardless.
  • Model selector: if given a choice of models, the default is usually the best balance of speed and quality for everyday use.

What Claude is bad at (honestly)

  • No native image generation — for that, use ChatGPT or Gemini; see our image generator roundup.
  • Smaller ecosystem — fewer third-party integrations and custom-bot marketplaces than ChatGPT.
  • Tighter free daily limit — heavy users hit the message cap faster than on Gemini's free tier.
  • Confident mistakes — like every AI, it can state wrong facts fluently. Verify names, numbers and citations.

FAQ

Is Claude free? Yes — the free tier includes Projects, Artifacts and file uploads, limited by a daily message cap. Claude Pro (~$20/month) raises limits and adds stronger models.

Claude or ChatGPT? Full breakdown in ChatGPT vs Claude — short version: Claude for writing, analysis and code; ChatGPT for the widest feature set.

Claude or Gemini? See Claude vs Gemini — Claude for depth and quality, Gemini for Google integration and free image generation.
